## 4.5 Directly Relevant Capability: Philadelphia Permitting and Licensing

The single most relevant qualification for this engagement is our **ten-year active relationship with the City of Philadelphia Department of Licenses and Inspections (L&I)**.

Since 2016, TWC has designed, built, and continuously maintained the public-facing licensing and inspections portal that serves approximately 85,000 active business and trade licenses. That portal:

- Hosts **multiple application types** with distinct conditional workflows routing applicants based on license category, business type, and submitted information—directly analogous to OCRSE's eight distinct event types
- Generates and distributes **permits and licenses** directly through the system
- Supports **post-submission document upload** and application modification under workflow controls
- Provides **real-time status tracking** for applicants and L&I staff
- Integrates with **billing, payment processing, and City business licensing databases**
- Supports **multi-user access** with configurable role-based permissions for L&I staff, supervisors, and external applicants
- Runs on **City of Philadelphia infrastructure and networks**, configured within existing City equipment and network environments without requiring hardware procurement under the contract
- Conforms to **City of Philadelphia addressing standards**, Century Date Standard, and WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA accessibility requirements

We did not acquire this understanding of Philadelphia's permitting environment from a sales engagement. We built it over nine years of active, continuous delivery work—and our current L&I contract runs through 2027. We know how the City's networks perform, how its procurement and change-order processes function, how interdepartmental coordination actually unfolds, and where the integration surfaces with payment processors, GIS, and business licensing systems are located and how they behave.

No other bidder on this procurement can offer equivalent institutional knowledge of Philadelphia's specific permitting infrastructure.
