## Proposed Scope of Work

### Phase I — Initiation *(Months 1–3)*

TWC will conduct a structured **Document Inventory and System Survey** across six identified platforms: PHLContracts, ACIS (Oracle), FAMIS (mainframe), ADPICs (mainframe), SharePoint, shared drives, and OnePhilly Oracle. Our data engineering team possesses direct experience in Oracle and mainframe data extraction, while our procurement advisory practice has successfully performed similar inventories for NJ Transit and Camden County.

**Deliverables include:**
- A **Current State Diagnostic Report** that catalogs all documents by type, department, creation date, expiry, and revision history.
- A **Template Index** classifying templates as *existing*, *existing requiring revision*, or *new*, including all attachments, exhibits, and disclosures.
- A **Document Rationalization Plan** that specifies a logical, searchable storage architecture with cross-referencing capabilities.
- A **Scope Validation Memo** with written recommendations on document management scope and priorities before the commencement of Phase II.

*Risk Mitigation:* Since the total document volume is uncertain until the inventory is completed, our fixed-price structure allocates a four-week discovery sprint with a defined Scope Validation gate. If findings materially exceed baseline assumptions, TWC will present options and cost implications for review, thus preserving the City's budget authority and avoiding unexpected costs.

### Phase II — Design *(Months 3–7)*

In this phase, TWC will develop **Template Structure Models** for approximately 15 procurement categories (including Goods, Vehicles, IT Hardware, and more) using a **clause library architecture** that classifies clauses as fixed or variable, while consolidating redundant language across document types.

Deliverables will include a **System Recommendations Report** that evaluates document storage, search, and clause library management across PHLContracts, ACIS, SharePoint, and Workday Strategic Sourcing. Our assessment will specifically address the limitations of Workday's clause library and provide vendor-neutral recommendations for subsequent procurement.

### Phase III — Creation *(Months 6–11)*

During this phase, TWC will conduct **clause-by-clause content development** for all new and revised templates, including standard fallback and alternative clause content for each variable clause. An updated **Document Index** will be produced upon completion of each category group, culminating in a final consolidated index by the end of Phase III.

Phases II and III will overlap by approximately six weeks to maintain momentum while ensuring the integrity of Phase II designs.
