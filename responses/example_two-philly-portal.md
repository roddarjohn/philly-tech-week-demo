## Our Understanding of the Work

The OPAL Project requires a trusted partner to (1) inventory and rationalize hundreds of procurement documents scattered across PHLContracts, ACIS, FAMIS, ADPICs, SharePoint, shared drives, and OnePhilly Oracle; (2) design a clause-library-based template architecture compatible with Workday Strategic Sourcing; and (3) produce publication-ready documents and templates across approximately 19 procurement categories — all within a fixed-price, milestone-driven contract aligned to the FY28 Workday go-live. TWC is structured precisely for this kind of work: we combine deep Workday ERP familiarity, procurement advisory experience, and the document-engineering discipline required to build durable, maintainable clause libraries at scale.

---

## Proposed Approach

**Phase I — Initiation (June–August 2026):** TWC will conduct a systematic inventory of all document sources, producing a complete Document Inventory & Audit and a Template Rationalization Index that designates each item as *existing*, *existing requiring revision*, or *new*. We will deliver a Current State Diagnostic Report and recommend the validated final document scope before any content is developed — eliminating downstream rework risk. *Quick Wins will be reported as discovered, not deferred.*

**Phase II — Design (August–September 2026):** We will develop Template Structure Models for each of the ~19 procurement categories, build a controlled clause library architecture, evaluate Workday Strategic Sourcing clause library suitability (acknowledging the platform's current limitations), and deliver a System Recommendations Report alongside a Future State Organization Model and Governance Framework Recommendation.

**Phase III — Creation (October–December 2026):** TWC will develop all new and revised documents and templates on a clause-by-clause basis, including standard alternates and fallback content, and deliver the regenerated Document Index. All content will be structured for searchability, cross-referencing by type, department, expiry date, and revision history.

**Phase IV — Closeout (January–April 2027):** Final Executive Report and presentation to the Executive Project Team; retrospective Quick Wins summary; transition documentation.

## Scope Risk Management

TWC recognizes that a fixed-price contract over an inventory of unknown size is the primary commercial risk in this engagement. Our mitigation: Phase I ends with a formal Scope Validation checkpoint at which TWC and the City jointly confirm the final document universe before Phase II and III pricing is locked. If the inventory materially exceeds agreed parameters, TWC will surface that finding immediately with cost and schedule options — not at contract end.
