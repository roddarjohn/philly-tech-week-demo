## SECTION 1: REQUIREMENTS

### 1.1 Functional Requirements (Appendix A.1)

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|----|-------------|----------|
| A.1.1 | Multi-application hosting — support multiple permit application types and concurrent management | Mandatory |
| A.1.2 | Permit generation and distribution — create, manage, and issue permits directly through system | Mandatory |
| A.1.3 | Service request creation — generate service requests/tasks based on applicant needs/inputs | Mandatory |
| A.1.4 | Post-submission editing and document upload — modify submitted applications and upload documentation after submission (subject to workflow controls) | Mandatory |
| A.1.5 | Conditional logic workflows — configurable workflows and conditional logic adapting routing, required fields, and documentation based on application inputs | Mandatory |
| A.1.6 | Real-time status tracking — transparent, real-time status updates for applicants and City personnel | Mandatory |
| A.1.7 | External communication — direct messaging between applicants and City with retained message history | Mandatory |
| A.1.8 | Internal collaboration tools — communication/collaboration features for interdepartmental coordination | Mandatory |
| A.1.9 | Centralized dashboards and calendars — unified views of applications, events, and schedules | Mandatory |
| A.1.10 | Third-party integrations — integration with billing/payment, GIS, business licensing databases, and supplemental applications via API, SSO, webhooks, file exchange, etc. | Mandatory |
| A.1.11 | Invoice management — tools for generating and managing invoices | Mandatory |
| A.1.12 | Multi-user access and permissions — multiple user profiles with configurable permission levels/roles | Mandatory |

## SECTION 3: RISKS

### 3.1 Timeline and Delivery Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Detail |
|------|-----------|--------|--------|
| Compressed implementation timeline | High | High | Proposal submission March 17; anticipated project start May 15, 2026 — aligns with MLB All-Star Game and FIFA World Cup surge in 2026 |
| Major event surge during implementation | High | High | MLB All-Star Game and FIFA World Cup in 2026 create peak demand precisely when system is being implemented |
| Schedule slippage affecting go-live | Medium | High | City reserves right to alter schedule; no guaranteed dates beyond submission deadline |
| Reliability period failure | Medium | High | 30-day consecutive defect-free reliability period required before final acceptance; failure triggers new 30-day period or potential contract termination |

### 3.2 Technical and Integration Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Detail |
|------|-----------|--------|--------|
| Integration complexity with existing City systems | High | High | Must integrate with payment processors, GIS, business licensing databases; City uses multiple existing systems across departments |
| Performance on existing City infrastructure | Medium | High | System installed on existing City equipment; proposals must specify performance standards in current environment; upgrades not covered under this contract |
| Data migration complexity | Medium | Medium | Transition from 8-page PDF email-based system to digital platform; historical data migration approach unclear |
| Interoperability with legacy systems | Medium | High | Multiple City departments involved; cross-departmental coordination requirements create complex integration landscape |
| Network/bandwidth constraints | Medium | Medium | Must specify bandwidth requirements; existing network may be a limiting factor |
| WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility compliance | Medium | Medium | All public-facing components must meet accessibility standards; retrofitting after deployment is costly |

### 3.3 Contractual and Financial Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Detail |
|------|-----------|--------|--------|
| Fixed price cost overruns | Medium | High | All costs must be fixed price; no time-and-materials allowed; scope creep not billable without Change Order |
| 20% retainage until final acceptance | High | Medium | City retains 20% of all payments until final acceptance — cash flow risk for vendor |
| 10% liquidated damages for campaign contribution violations | Low | High | Material misstatements in disclosures trigger 10% of contract value as liquidated damages; contract voidable |
| Contract voidable for demographic disclosure misstatements | Low | High | Material misstatements under 2+ contracts constitute breach; contract voidable and subject to liquidated damages |
| Post-implementation scope changes pricing model | Medium | Medium | City must understand whether changes require vendor or can be made by staff; pricing model (hourly, subscription, tiered) must be defined upfront |
| Options/alternates cost uncertainty | Medium | Medium | Any functionality not in base system must be separately priced; City may choose not to purchase add-ons |
